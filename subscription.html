Frequently Asked Questions / Terms and Conditions

Where is the estate located?

Sardius Estate is located at Odo-Asoyin/Isiwo, Ijebu-Ode, Odogbolu Local Government, Ogun State.

Who owns / develops the estate?

Gtext Land.

What type of title do you have on the land?

Registered Survey.

Are there any encumbrances on the land?

The land is free from government acquisition and adverse claims.

What is the price of the land?

$9,714.90 (N15,000,000).

Is there a payment plan?

The standard payment plan is 3 months in installments: 30% of the total agreed amount on the first installment, 25% on the second installment (first month), 25% on the third installment (second month), and 20% on the final installment (third month). Each extra month needed adds a 5% charge to the total amount. This should be explained in the contract of sale.

What is the size of a plot?

400 sqm.

What other payment is required apart from the land price?

No further payments are required except those necessary for perfection of title, which may vary with statutory government fees. The purchaser shall also pay developmental fees of $8,333.33 (N12,500,000), which can be spread over 12 to 36 months, for estate improvements and other developmental obligations during the land-banking period. Infrastructure fees or service charges may also be payable when construction begins.

What infrastructure is expected?

Drainage systems, electrification and street lighting, water supply, waste management, security cameras, paved roads, and perimeter fencing.

What do I get after the initial land payment?

A receipt for payments made.

Is the road to the estate accessible?

Yes, the road to the estate is accessible.

When will my plot(s) be allocated?

Plots will be allocated after full payment. Upon purchase, a temporary allocation will be assigned and sent to you. You have 3 months to complete payment, after which your allocation will be made permanent.

What is the size and price of a corner piece?

The size varies; the amount is prorated using the price per square meter at the point of purchase.

Can I start construction immediately?

Construction can commence once land payment is complete and the survey, title deeds, and building approval have been obtained.

Are commercial plots available?

Yes.

Are there building restrictions?

All building designs must be submitted to Gtext Land, approved by Gtext Homes in the first instance, and then by the Ogun State Government before building work can commence.

Can I have a survey for more than one plot?

Yes, additional surveys attract additional costs.

Can I pay an agent?

No. All payments should be made to the Gtext Land account at its designated banks. Cheques should be issued in favour of Gtext Land. The company accepts no responsibility for liability arising from deviation from this instruction.

Can I get a refund if I cannot continue payments?

Refer to the refund policy below.

Can I resell my plot / property?

Yes. Subscribers up to date on land payments, documentation levy, and development levy can resell their plots, subject to approval by Gtext Land management. The reseller must provide the buyer’s details so the company can update its database.

Refund Policy

No refunds on completed land purchases

All land purchases completed and confirmed by Gtext Land Limited are final and non-refundable. Once a purchase has been concluded and the land allocated to the purchaser, payments shall not be refunded because the purchaser changes their mind, decides not to proceed, or is no longer interested. Purchasers are advised to conduct necessary inspections and ensure they are satisfied with the property and its terms before completing payment.

Buyback Option Clause

Where a client is no longer interested in maintaining ownership of purchased property, the client may seek a buyer or request that the Company repurchase the property, subject to these terms.

  1. Buyback option. If the Company repurchases the property, the repurchase price is reduced by 35% of the initial purchase sum agreed when the property was purchased.
  2. Payment terms. Payment is issued in staggered installments, commencing 90 days after signing the Deed of Assignment. Amounts and timing are mutually agreed in a separate agreement.
  3. Company discretion. The Company may decide whether to proceed with a buyback and may discontinue the option based on market conditions, financial considerations, or other relevant factors.

Resell Clause

  1. Sell option. Previous buyers may independently sell the property. Upon securing a buyer, they must obtain a release form to proceed with resale, even if the land or house is not fully paid.
  2. Full payment option. The new buyer pays the full purchase price directly to GTEXT LAND as seller.
  3. Payment split option (optional). If the new buyer makes a partial payment, the initial owner completes the balance to GTEXT LAND as seller.

Release Clause

Once agreed payments and terms are fulfilled, GTEXT LAND transfers ownership rights from the initial purchaser to the new buyer. Both parties agree to collaborate on a smooth transfer. This is a general template; additional clauses or modifications may be needed for a specific resale agreement. Consult a legal professional for a comprehensive customized agreement.

Default Clause

The company reserves the right to revoke the subscription where the client defaults on the payment plan for three months; the property will be deemed forfeited.

Revocation Clause

The client agrees that the company may revoke the plot(s) if there is a default in installment payments or any fees mentioned above. Costs incurred as a result of revocation shall be borne by the defaulting party.

Reallocation Clause

The Company may reallocate the Client to another estate if issues affect the subscribed plots, including plot availability or zoning. If the Client does not meet the payment schedule before all available plots are sold, the Company may reallocate the Client to another estate with available plots, subject to that estate’s terms and pricing.

Ratification of Title

Each client is solely responsible for ratification and procurement of their individual Governor’s Consent under applicable laws. The Company is not liable for related delays, costs, or obligations. Clients must ensure compliance with legal and administrative requirements and bear the attendant costs.
